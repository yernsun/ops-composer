# Paid professional services

[简体中文](COMMERCIAL_SERVICES.zh-CN.md) | English

The project steward may separately offer deployment, migration, and security-hardening services.
Availability is not guaranteed. This page is an operating policy and an invitation to discuss
scope; it is not a binding offer, quotation, warranty, or SLA.

## Potential scope

A separately priced engagement may include:

- deployment design, environment readiness review, installation, and configuration;
- data, configuration, Playbook, or version migration planning and execution; and
- threat modeling, configuration review, secrets handling, TLS/reverse-proxy hardening,
  least-privilege review, backup/restore exercises, and remediation planning.

Penetration testing, certification, legal-compliance attestation, 24x7 operations, monitoring,
incident response, on-call support, custom development, ongoing patching, and an SLA are excluded
unless a signed agreement explicitly includes them.

## Written agreement required

No work starts and no obligation arises until the customer and the actual service provider sign a
written master agreement, statement of work, or order form. At minimum it should identify:

- the legal parties, authorized signers, governing law, and notices/contact details;
- scope, assumptions, dependencies, exclusions, deliverables, milestones, and acceptance criteria;
- fees, currency, invoicing, taxes, expenses, cancellation, and change control;
- customer responsibilities, access approvals, backup/rollback duties, and maintenance windows;
- confidentiality, personal-data processing, credential handling, retention, and deletion;
- intellectual-property treatment for customer-specific work and third-party materials;
- any warranty, support window, SLA/service credits, liability cap, and indemnities; and
- security-incident escalation and handover requirements.

Do not send production credentials or customer data before those controls are agreed. Use
short-lived, least-privilege access and customer-controlled audit trails whenever practical.

## Relationship to open source and sponsorship

Paying for services does not change the license of OpsComposer. The customer must still comply
with `AGPL-3.0-only` and all third-party licenses unless it separately obtains a valid commercial
software license. GitHub Sponsors or PayPal funding payments are not deposits, retainers, invoices,
or prepayment for these services.

Customer-specific deliverables must state whether they are (a) changes to AGPL-covered code,
(b) customer-owned material, or (c) provider pre-existing material. A services provider cannot
grant proprietary rights in third-party code it does not own. Upstream contributions remain
subject to [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the active CLA process, if any.

## Commercial software licensing

No commercial software license is available merely because this page exists. A future
dual-license offering requires a separate signed agreement and a rights audit covering every
relevant contribution and dependency. It can provide an additional license for project-owned
code, but cannot revoke an AGPL license already granted or override third-party licenses.
